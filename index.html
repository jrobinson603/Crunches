How to use the plan

Complete the prescribed sets with 45–90 seconds of rest between sets. Keep the movement controlled; stop a set when your form breaks down. The listed reps are a target, not a reason to strain.

Form & safety: Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat. Cross arms over your chest or place fingertips lightly by your temples. Curl your shoulders up using your abs, then lower slowly. Don’t pull your neck or use momentum. Stop for sharp pain, especially in your neck or back.

If you cannot complete a target with good form, do fewer reps and repeat that week before progressing. Crunches train abdominal endurance; they are not a substitute for balanced strength training.
